My story

From medicine to natural hygiene

I trained as a doctor (MBBS) in India. During my studies and practice, I saw that most chronic problems were managed rather than resolved. I began searching for the root causes of disease — and found natural hygiene.

Natural hygiene teaches that the body is always working towards health. When we supply what it needs — fresh ripe fruits and greens, clean water, sunlight, fresh air, movement, rest and peace of mind — and remove what harms it, the body heals itself.

I live this way myself as a raw vegan and fruitarian, and today I guide my patients to make diet and lifestyle changes that are simple, joyful and sustainable.

Eat Fruits & Heal is my way of sharing this knowledge — through consultations, recipes, articles and video lectures.
